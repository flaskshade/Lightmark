Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026

Lightmark is designed to be a simple and local Mac app. It does not require an account and does not collect analytics or telemetry.

Your files

Your Markdown files stay on your Mac. Lightmark reads and edits them locally and does not upload their contents anywhere.

App data

Lightmark stores basic preferences and recent-file information locally on your Mac. This information is not sent to us.

Website

The Lightmark website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand basic traffic, such as pageviews, visits, approximate countries, and browser or device types. It does not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, or browser fingerprinting.

Like most websites, Cloudflare, our hosting and analytics provider, may receive basic technical information such as your IP address and browser information when you visit the site. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy for its practices.

External content

Links you open from Lightmark or its website may lead to other services with their own privacy policies.

If a Markdown file contains an image hosted online, loading that image may connect directly to the website hosting it.

Contact

If you contact us, we receive whatever information you choose to include in your message and use it only to respond to you.

Changes

If Lightmark's privacy practices change, this page will be updated accordingly.

Questions? Visit the Contact page.
