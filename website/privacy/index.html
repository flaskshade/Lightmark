Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2, 2026

Lightmark is a free macOS utility for opening, reading, and editing Markdown. This policy describes the current app and website.

Your documents

Your documents are read and edited on your Mac. Lightmark does not upload their contents to our servers, require an account, or include analytics or telemetry.

Local preferences

The app stores preferences and recent-file information locally on your Mac. The website’s editable demo stays in your browser and resets when you reload the page.

The website

This website currently includes no analytics scripts or advertising trackers. When the website is hosted publicly, its hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP addresses, requested pages, and browser details to deliver and secure the site. We will update this policy with the provider and any analytics we introduce before launch.

External services

External links, including our X profile, open services with their own privacy policies. Remote images referenced by a Markdown document may connect to the service hosting that image.

Contacting us

If you contact Flaskshade, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Please avoid sharing private document contents unless necessary. The contact service’s own privacy policy also applies.

Changes and questions

We will update this page when the app or website’s data practices change. For questions or requests concerning information you have shared with us, email team@trylightmark.com or visit our Contact page.
